How I design

From learning objectives to useful action

01

Start with the performance need

Identify what learners need to do differently, then define observable outcomes.

02

Practice in context

Use realistic decisions, guided workflows, and purposeful interactions—not interaction for its own sake.

03

Check for application

Align feedback and assessment to the choices and tasks learners encounter at work.

About the designer

Bridging learning, technology, and organizational change

My professional background spans learning and development, instructional design, technology training, and large-scale digital adoption. I bring an educator's focus on learner needs together with an enterprise mindset for implementation and performance.
